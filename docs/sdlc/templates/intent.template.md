# Intent: <one line, what is wanted>

## What

What is wanted, in two or three sentences. Concrete enough that someone could
tell whether it exists yet.

## Why now

The reason this is worth doing before the other things on the board. If the
honest answer is "it was next in the list", that is a signal to reconsider.

## Constraints

What binds this: promises we have already made to readers, DS-first ownership,
the publishing bar, privacy commitments, anything that rules out an otherwise
obvious approach.

## How we will know it worked

The signal that should move, named as a specific metric or event, with the
tier it sits in and the threshold that would count as evidence.

At low volume, prefer **counting rare valuable events** over rates — a
percentage computed from twelve events is not a percentage.

If no signal can move within the measurement window, say so here. An
initiative that cannot be evaluated is allowed, but it must be labelled, not
disguised as one that can.

## Not doing

The adjacent work this deliberately excludes, so scope creep has to be a
decision rather than a drift.
