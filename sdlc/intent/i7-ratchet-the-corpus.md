## Claim

Every published article clears 9.5 on all five lenses, or is retired.

## Audience

Readers. This is the one intent here whose output they see.

## Why us

Measured corpus mean is 5.7/10 against a stated floor of 9.5. Half the corpus
draws 22% of all views; nine "Getting Started" guides have drawn zero
engagement between them. The floor is not aspirational — articles below it
actively signal that Interlace lints are low-effort, which is the opposite of
what the corpus exists to demonstrate.

## Evidence we believe exists

- [x] 83 published articles, 0 scored under the new chain
- [x] A prior scorecard covering 42 of them (2026-05-29), usable as a triage prior but not as a current score
- [x] Known duplicate sets already triaged: SonarJS ×2, Microsoft-SDL ×2, import-next ×3

## Kill criterion

Per article, not per intent: if a piece cannot reach 9.5 without becoming an
article we would not have chosen to write, it is **retired**, not rewritten.
Retirement is a success outcome here.

## Dependencies

Downstream of I-1 and I-4. A corpus you cannot measure cannot be ratcheted —
which is precisely why 5.7 has not moved since May.

## Approval note

Scoring all 83 is a large fan-out with real token cost. It needs explicit
authorisation before running, not assumption. Status stays `proposed` until
Ofri approves.
